## Screening matters
Diabetes first recognized during pregnancy is often found through screening rather than symptoms. Attend the tests recommended during antenatal care, even when you feel well, and share any previous diabetes or pregnancy history with the health worker.

## Care is personal
High blood sugar in pregnancy can affect both the pregnant person and baby. A health worker can make a plan using the person’s test results, food pattern, activity, monitoring needs and, when appropriate, medicines. Do not start or stop medicine without advice.

## After pregnancy
Diabetes during pregnancy can increase the chance of diabetes later in life. Ask the maternity team about follow-up testing and healthy routines after birth. Urgent symptoms or a very high test result should be discussed promptly with a health worker.
