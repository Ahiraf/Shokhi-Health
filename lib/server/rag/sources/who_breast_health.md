## Breast changes
Breasts naturally change across the menstrual cycle, pregnancy, breastfeeding, and later life. Tenderness or fullness can have several causes. A new or lasting change should be discussed with a health worker rather than diagnosed at home.

## Changes worth checking
A new lump, a change in the size or shape of one breast, skin pulling or dimpling, a change in the nipple, or unusual nipple fluid can need assessment. These signs do not prove cancer, but they should not be ignored.

## Getting help
A health worker can ask about the change and decide whether an examination or another test is appropriate. People should be able to seek care without shame or blame. Earlier assessment can make it easier to understand the cause and choose care.

## Practical reminder
Notice what is normal for your own body, including changes around your period. Seek prompt care for a rapidly growing change, bloody nipple fluid, skin breakdown, or severe illness.
