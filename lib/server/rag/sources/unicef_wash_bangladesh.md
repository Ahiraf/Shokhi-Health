## Safe water and hygiene
UNICEF Bangladesh notes that water quality, sanitation, handwashing, and climate-related disruption remain important concerns. Safe water decisions should follow local testing, government, and health guidance.

## Girls' dignity and resilience
Girls and women need privacy, water, clean menstrual materials, and safe sanitation. Floods, salinity, and damaged water points can disrupt these services, so households and schools benefit from practical preparedness plans.
