## Keeping children learning
UNICEF Bangladesh works toward education that is inclusive, relevant, accessible, and resilient during emergencies and climate disruption. Children who face poverty, disability, disaster, child labour, or child marriage may need extra support to stay in or return to learning.

## Adolescents and skills
Education for adolescents can include transferable life skills and alternative learning pathways for children who are out of school. Digital access should be paired with safety, inclusion, and support for children with limited connectivity.
