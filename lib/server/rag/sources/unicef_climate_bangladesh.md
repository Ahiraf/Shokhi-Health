## Heat safety
Children, pregnant women, older people, and people with health conditions can be vulnerable during heatwaves. Staying in a cooler place, drinking safe water, wearing light clothing, and avoiding the hottest hours can reduce risk.

## Preparedness
Families can prepare safe water, food, medicines, menstrual supplies, a torch, and important documents before a disaster. Follow official warnings and move early to a safe place when instructed.
