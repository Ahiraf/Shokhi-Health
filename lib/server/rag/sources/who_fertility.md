## What infertility means
Infertility is a condition of the reproductive system in which pregnancy does not happen after a period of regular unprotected sex. It can affect people of any gender and can involve the woman, the man, both partners, or an unexplained cause.

## Possible contributors
Causes can include problems affecting the reproductive organs, hormones, infections, or other health conditions. Age, past illness, medicines, and menstrual history may be relevant. Only an assessment can identify the cause.

## Getting an assessment
A health worker may ask about both partners and suggest tests based on the history. Do not buy unproven medicines or blame one partner. Reproductive health care should be respectful and confidential.

## Emotional support
Pressure, stigma, and blame can cause distress. Partners and families can help by listening, protecting privacy, and supporting a planned visit with a qualified health worker.
