## The cervix and HPV
The cervix is the lower part of the womb. Human papillomavirus, or HPV, is common. Most infections clear naturally, but some types can persist and cause changes in the cervix over time.

## Prevention and screening
HPV vaccination and cervical screening can help prevent cervical cancer where they are available. Health workers can explain which services are offered locally and who should receive them. Follow local health guidance rather than relying on rumours.

## Symptoms to check
Bleeding after sex, bleeding between periods, unusual discharge, or persistent pelvic symptoms should be discussed with a health worker. These symptoms have many possible causes and do not confirm a diagnosis.

## Respectful care
People deserve private, respectful information and care. Do not delay because of embarrassment. A health worker can decide whether an examination or test is needed.
