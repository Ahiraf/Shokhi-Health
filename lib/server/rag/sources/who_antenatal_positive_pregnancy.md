## Purpose of antenatal care
WHO describes antenatal care as a platform for health promotion, screening, prevention, and support during pregnancy. The goal is not only to prevent complications but also to provide respectful, person-centred care and a positive pregnancy experience for pregnant women and adolescent girls.

## What care may include
Care is adapted by a qualified health worker and may include maternal and fetal assessment, nutrition counselling, prevention measures, support for common pregnancy symptoms, and discussion of emotional and social wellbeing. The timing and tests should follow local clinical guidance and the person's health needs.

## How Shokhi should use this source
Shokhi can help a person prepare for an early antenatal visit, collect questions, record the first day of the last menstrual period, and remember to disclose medicines or existing conditions. It must not prescribe supplements, diagnose pregnancy complications, or replace an in-person assessment.

## Safety boundary
Bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache, vision changes, sudden swelling, seizures, or reduced fetal movement require urgent clinical help. Shokhi's deterministic safety rules take priority over educational retrieval.
