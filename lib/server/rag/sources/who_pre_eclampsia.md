## What it is
Pre-eclampsia is a pregnancy complication involving high blood pressure and signs that organs such as the kidneys or liver may be affected. It can threaten the health of the pregnant person and baby, so antenatal care and blood-pressure checks are important.

## Warning signs
Possible warning signs include a severe headache, changes in vision, pain in the upper abdomen, sudden swelling of the face or hands, or feeling very unwell. Some people have no obvious symptoms, which is why routine antenatal checks matter.

## Getting help
Do not wait for a home diagnosis or start medicine without a clinician’s advice. Contact a maternity service promptly for warning signs. Seizures, loss of consciousness, severe breathing difficulty or rapidly worsening illness require emergency hospital care.
