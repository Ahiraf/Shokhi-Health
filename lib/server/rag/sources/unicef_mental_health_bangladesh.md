## Supportive environments
Young people's mental wellbeing is supported by safe relationships, caring adults, inclusive schools, time to rest, and access to appropriate help. Listening without shame or blame makes it easier for a child or adolescent to ask for support.

## Urgent concerns
Persistent distress, inability to function, self-harm thoughts, or immediate danger need prompt support from a qualified professional or emergency service. A young person in crisis should not be left alone.
