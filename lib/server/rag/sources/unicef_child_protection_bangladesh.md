## Community support
UNICEF-supported child protection community hubs in Bangladesh bring together families, schools, communities, and social workers. Their work includes preventing violence and child marriage, supporting birth registration, and helping children access safer services.

## Safe response
A child or adolescent who reports harm should be listened to, believed, and connected with trained support. Do not pressure the child to confront an unsafe person alone. Immediate danger requires emergency help and a safe place.
