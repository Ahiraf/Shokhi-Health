## The first six weeks after birth
WHO's postnatal guideline brings together routine care recommendations for women and newborns during the first six weeks after birth. It describes a positive postnatal experience as one where women, babies, partners, parents, and caregivers receive understandable information, reassurance, and support in a respectful, person-centred way.

## Practical support
Postnatal care can include assessment of recovery, support for feeding, mental and emotional wellbeing, family-planning counselling, and help understanding newborn care. The exact schedule and services depend on local health-system guidance and the woman's or baby's needs.

## How Shokhi should use this source
Shokhi can help a new mother prepare questions, notice changes in mood, ask for support, and discuss birth spacing with a health worker. It must not diagnose postpartum depression, infection, or newborn illness.

## Safety boundary
Heavy bleeding, fever, foul-smelling discharge, severe pain, breathing difficulty, or thoughts of self-harm require urgent help. Shokhi's deterministic safety rules take priority over educational retrieval.
