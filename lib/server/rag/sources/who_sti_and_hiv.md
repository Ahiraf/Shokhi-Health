## What STIs are
Sexually transmitted infections can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or parasites. Some can be cured and others can be managed with treatment. Some infections cause no symptoms, so symptoms alone cannot rule them out.

## Possible signs
Unusual discharge, sores, genital itching, burning when urinating, pelvic pain, or pain during sex can have several causes. A health worker can provide confidential advice, testing, and treatment when needed.

## Prevention
Correct and consistent condom use reduces the risk of many STIs and unintended pregnancy. Vaccination can prevent some infections where it is recommended and available. Ask a health worker for practical, private guidance.

## When to seek care
Prompt care matters for fever, severe lower-abdominal pain, sores, pregnancy with a possible infection, or a partner known to have an STI. Avoid blame and seek care for both partners when a health worker recommends it.
