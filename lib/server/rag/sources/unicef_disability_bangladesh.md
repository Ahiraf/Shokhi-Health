## Inclusion
Children with disabilities should be visible, counted, and included in health care, education, recreation, social protection, and decisions about their lives. Services need coordination, accessibility, trained workers, and respect for the person's communication and preferences.

## Family support
Families can ask services about reasonable accommodations such as accessible entrances, plain language, sign support, communication assistance, or extra time. Stigma, neglect, confinement, and violence are protection concerns.
