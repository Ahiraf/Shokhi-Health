## Recovery after birth
The weeks after birth are important for the mother's physical and emotional recovery and for the baby's health. Rest, food, fluids, hygiene, practical help, and planned postnatal contact all support recovery.

## Feeding support
Breastfeeding can take time to learn. A trained health worker can help with positioning, pain, concerns about feeding, and questions about the baby's health. A mother should be supported without pressure or blame.

## Emotional wellbeing
Tiredness and changes in mood can happen after birth. Ongoing sadness, fear, anxiety, hopelessness, or difficulty functioning should be shared with a trusted person and a health worker.

## Warning signs
Heavy bleeding, fever, severe pain, fainting, breathing difficulty, or feeling very unwell after birth needs prompt medical care. In an emergency in Bangladesh, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital.
