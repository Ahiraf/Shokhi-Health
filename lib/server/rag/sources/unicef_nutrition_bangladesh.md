## A varied diet
UNICEF Bangladesh describes nutrition as important for growth, learning, immunity, and lifelong health. Families benefit from varied foods that may include vegetables, fruit, pulses, eggs, fish, or meat, according to availability and local advice.

## Anaemia and support
Anaemia and micronutrient deficiencies affect children, adolescents, pregnant women, and mothers. Weakness, persistent tiredness, dizziness, or breathlessness should be discussed with a qualified health worker. Supplement decisions should follow local clinical guidance rather than self-treatment.
