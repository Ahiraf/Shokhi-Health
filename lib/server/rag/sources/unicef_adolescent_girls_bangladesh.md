## Life skills, education, and participation
UNICEF Bangladesh highlights the importance of adolescent health services, education, life skills, digital literacy, nutrition, and girls' participation in decisions that affect them. Young people should be treated with respect and given safe, age-appropriate information.

## Child marriage and violence
Child marriage and violence can interrupt education, limit choices, and create health risks. Prevention requires trusted adults, communities, services, and the meaningful participation of girls. A girl facing pressure or harm should be supported without blame and connected to safe help.
