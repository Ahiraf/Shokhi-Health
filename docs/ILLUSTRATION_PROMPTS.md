# Shokhi illustration prompts

Use the existing Shokhi mascot as the visual reference for every prompt. Keep the same warm
face, expressive brown eyes, black braided hair, soft pink clothing, sage-green scarf, rounded
3D-cartoon proportions, gentle lighting, and rose/sage palette. Keep the images educational,
respectful, age-appropriate, non-sexual, and free from shame or fear. Do not place medical text,
labels, logos, watermarks, or UI inside the illustration; add those in the app separately.

## Shared character direction

```text
Use the supplied Shokhi mascot reference and preserve her identity, face, hairstyle, braid
silhouette, clothing palette, friendly expression, and polished 3D storybook-cartoon style.
Create a culturally respectful Bangladesh setting with soft rose, cream, and sage colors, warm
diffused light, simple uncluttered composition, clear body language, and one emotionally positive
learning moment. Leave generous negative space on one side for interface copy. No text, no logos,
no watermark, no photorealism, no medical gore, no fear-based imagery, no sexualized posing.
```

## First period

```text
An age-appropriate younger version of the same Shokhi mascot, a cheerful Bangladeshi girl in a
simple pink salwar kameez and sage scarf, sitting calmly with a trusted female adult in a bright
bedroom. A small closed sanitary-pad pouch, clean underwear, and a notebook are visible as gentle
learning objects. The adult is listening kindly while Shokhi looks reassured and curious. Show
privacy, dignity, and confidence around a first period. Soft rose and cream background, a few
small flowers and stars, generous empty space on the left for UI copy, polished 3D storybook
illustration, no text or labels.
```
